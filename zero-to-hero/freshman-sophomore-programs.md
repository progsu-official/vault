## if you're coming straight from high school

The adjustment that matters most isn't the coursework, it's that nobody is checking whether you did the extra credit anymore. In high school, staying on track was mostly automatic: a counselor, a syllabus, a parent reminding you about a deadline. In college, none of that exists by default, and the students who fall behind in the recruiting race usually aren't the ones who struggle academically, they're the ones waiting for someone to tell them what to do next.

This stage (freshman and sophomore year) is where that gap either opens or closes. You have more runway than you'll ever have again, and the programs below exist specifically to hand early students the kind of structure and access that upperclassmen have to build for themselves. Use that runway on purpose.
