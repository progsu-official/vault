# junior year

The internship that matters most.

## focus

The internship that matters most. This is usually your best shot at a name-brand internship, since you'll be a rising senior applying to full-time-track roles next year.

## classes to prioritize

- algorithms, the real one, not just intro DSA, this is where interview-level pattern practice and coursework actually overlap
- database systems, useful for almost every backend-adjacent role
- upper-level electives that actually map to the role you're targeting, not just whatever fits your schedule

## how to step it up

Ramp LeetCode into real pattern practice, not just volume. Build one or two flagship projects you can talk through in depth in an interview. Start networking deliberately: career fairs, cold outreach, referrals.
