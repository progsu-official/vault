# freshman year

Foundations and exploration. The goal this year isn't landing an internship, it's building the base everything else stands on, and figuring out if you actually like this.

## focus

Exploration and foundations, not internships yet. Most freshmen don't land one, and that's normal.

## classes to prioritize

- your intro programming sequence, don't just pass it, actually understand it
- discrete math, it underlies almost everything in DSA and theory courses later
- if your program offers an early data structures course, take it as soon as you're eligible, it's one of the most useful classes for interview prep down the line

## how to step it up

Don't just take the classes, build something outside of them. One small project by the end of the year, it doesn't need to be impressive, it just needs to be yours.
