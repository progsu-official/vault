# sophomore year

Your first real internship, and picking a direction.

## focus

Your first real internship, and picking a direction. This is when programs like STEP, Meta University, Explore, APP, and Ignite matter most, see [[freshman-sophomore-programs]].

## classes to prioritize

- core data structures and algorithms, if you haven't already
- computer organization or systems, this becomes the foundation for anything backend, infra, or low-level later
- your first real elective in a direction you're curious about (web, ML, security, whatever), even if you're not sure yet

## how to step it up

Take on a project with actual scope, not a tutorial clone. Start applying broadly, including to less "prestigious" titles, just to get real interview reps.
