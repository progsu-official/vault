# senior year

Converting into a full-time offer.

## focus

Converting into a full-time offer. If junior year's internship went well, this year is about converting it into a return offer. If it didn't, or you want more options, this is your last full recruiting cycle before graduation, treat it seriously.

## classes to prioritize

- whatever capstone or systems-heavy elective your program offers, it's often the closest thing to real engineering work you'll do before a job
- anything you've been putting off that's actually a gap on your resume, not just an easy elective

## how to step it up

Stack multiple applications, lean on referrals over cold applying, and don't be afraid to use an off-cycle (spring) role to buy yourself another cycle of reps if this cycle doesn't land the way you want.
