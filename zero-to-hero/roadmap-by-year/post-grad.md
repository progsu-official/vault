# post-grad

Keep going, if your goal actually calls for it.

## focus

Most people go straight to full-time work after senior year, and for most goals that's the right move, there's no reason to delay it by default. This page is for the cases where it isn't: if your actual endgame is something like AI/ML research, quant, or maximizing comp as a dev over the long run, a bachelor's alone sometimes isn't enough runway to get there, and it can be worth more school, a specialized program, research experience, or simply holding out for the right opportunity instead of taking the first full-time offer available.

Whether that trade is worth it depends entirely on your own endgame, not a general rule, see [[picking-your-endgame]] if you haven't mapped that out yet. If your goal doesn't need it, don't manufacture a reason to delay working, the reps you get on the job are worth more than more classroom time in most cases.

Whichever path you take, the same thing is true once you're actually working: your first job is rarely your last. What matters from here is whether you're on a track that keeps increasing your options (comp, title, company brand) over time, not just whether you have "a job."

## how to step it up

Revisit [[picking-your-endgame]] once you're actually working or further into a specialized track, your read on macro vs micro will sharpen fast with real experience.
