# post-grad

Keep going, if your goal actually calls for it.

## focus

Most people go straight to full-time work after senior year, and for most goals that's the right move, so there's no reason to delay it by default. This page is for the cases where it isn't the right move. If your actual endgame is something like AI/ML research, quant, or maximizing comp as a dev over the long run, a bachelor's alone sometimes isn't enough runway to get there. Then it can be worth more school, a specialized program, research experience, or just holding out for the right opportunity instead of taking the first full-time offer available.

Whether that trade is worth it depends entirely on your own endgame, so treat it as a case-by-case call. See [[picking-your-endgame]] if you haven't mapped that out yet. If your goal doesn't need it, don't manufacture a reason to delay working, since the reps you get on the job are usually worth more than more classroom time.

Whichever path you take, the same thing is true once you're actually working. Your first job is rarely your last. What matters from here is whether you're on a track that keeps increasing your options over time (comp, title, company brand), so having "a job" on its own isn't the finish line.

## how to step it up

Revisit the endgame question once you're actually working, or once you're further into a specialized track. Your read on macro vs micro will sharpen fast with real experience.
